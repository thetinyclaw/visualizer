AIRPLAY TV MODE

  1. Open iPhone Control Center.
  2. Tap Screen Mirroring.
  3. Choose your Apple TV or AirPlay television.

Then rotate your phone sideways and enter TV Mode. Safari does not expose the AirPlay picker for a WebGL canvas, so mirroring is selected from Control Center.
